# Mission: Advanced Computer Architecture (Prof. Weber, Winter 2026)

## Why
Danyal is an AI engineer taking the ACA module, which follows Barlas' _Multicore & GPU Programming_. He needs to pass the first Moodle test on 19.11.2026 (slide sets 1 and 2 plus Seminar Exercise Sheet 1), and later the final exam. The same ideas explain the hardware his ML work runs on: why GPUs scale, and where parallel speedup stops.

## Success looks like
- Solves speedup, efficiency, Amdahl and Gustafson-Barsis calculations by hand, in the slide notation, without the formula sheet.
- Solves all three tasks of Exercise Sheet 1 unaided (Barlas 1.8, the 1-D vs 2-D inequality, the prefix-sum loop counts).
- Names and distinguishes PCAM steps, the six decomposition patterns (D1 to D6) and the program structure patterns, and picks a fitting one for a described problem.
- Passes the Moodle test on 19.11.2026, then the final exam.

## Constraints
- Five days until the first test (today is 14.11.2026). The test is a 21-minute Moodle test held during the lecture block; question types are still unknown.
- Background: Python, machine learning from scratch, generative AI. No prior computer architecture or parallel programming course.
- The professor's slides and notation are authoritative (for example, α is the parallelizable fraction).

## Out of scope
- Slide sets 3 onward (threads, OpenMP, MPI, GPU programming) until the first test is done.
- Classical processor design (logic gates, instruction sets, cache design): not what this module covers so far.
